📓 Travel log for printing

The travel log works for every place – print it and make copies. The place page is filled in at every place. The state page is filled in only at the first place the group visits in a state. Everything is written in English.

Tip: copy more place pages than state pages. Made for A4 paper and works fine on a black-and-white printer. Turn off “headers and footers” in the print window.
