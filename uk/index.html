Should they take the cheap coach to Manchester, the fast train to Edinburgh or the ferry across the Irish Sea to Belfast? They must find a place to stay, write the travel log and earn some money. Across the UK turns English, geography and money skills into one big race around Britain.

How the game works

The students work in groups of 3–4. Each group has a budget and starts in a random place in the UK. Everything they read and write is in English.

Hostel, inn or grand hotel? In the Scottish Highlands you can go wild camping for free – but only if you know the Outdoor Access Code. A better hotel gives better sleep and more energy to work.

Facts, sights and a drawing from the place – written in English. The teacher approves it – and the place is theirs.

Read the whole guide step by step
Before the first lessonabout 15 minutes
  1. Log in as a teacher.

    Press “Log in as a teacher” and use Google or email and password. The students do not need an account.

  2. Make a game.

    Give it a name, for example “7A autumn”. One game per class or period.

  3. Set the rules.

    Under Settings: starting money per student, points, when places are locked, how many jobs and quizzes per day, and start and end dates. The default values work fine.

  4. Make the groups.

    Under Groups: name, colour and number of students. Each group automatically gets a starting place, a group code and a PIN.

  5. Print.

    “Print login cards” (one per group) and “📓 Travel log” – it works for every place, so you can copy as many as you need.

  6. Do you want a smaller map?

    Under Places you can close places or whole regions – for example to play only in England, or only in Scotland.

In every lessonhow a lesson flows
  1. Open the board on the big screen.

    The board shows the map of the UK, the scoreboard, the latest news and the duels. You can also show the board right in the teacher portal with the button “📺 Board” – with a drawer for travel logs and duels.

  2. Share the link with the students.

    Under Overview → “Share with the students” you find the student link and a QR code. The students log in with the group code and PIN.

  3. The students travel.

    Find a place to stay → do the travel log → press “handed in” → work, take a quiz or travel on. On the way they spin a wheel and answer general knowledge questions in English: 2 correct for a direct flight, 4 with one stop – and more for trains, coaches and ferries, depending on the distance. The “Next step” box at the top always tells them what to do.

  4. Approve travel logs.

    Under Overview → “Waiting for you”. If the place is free, it becomes the group's. If another group owns it, there will be a duel.

  5. Run duels.

    Press “🎲 Draw a task” – the task spins onto the board. Start the countdown, and press the winner afterwards.

Good tipsfrom the classroom
  1. English all the way.

    The game, the quizzes, the jobs and the travel log are in simple English. Let the groups read out loud to each other, and keep a dictionary close. New words like coach, ferry and city centre come up again and again.

  2. Pounds, miles and British spelling.

    Prices are in pounds and distances in miles, and the game uses British words and spelling (colour, maths, coach). Compare with Across America, which uses American English.

  3. Run the game together.

    Under Settings → “Invite a teacher” you make a link for colleagues. They can approve, run duels and show the board.

  4. Use the pause button.

    Under Settings you can pause the game – then nobody can buy anything.

  5. Reward effort.

    “💰 Adjust money” on a group card gives (or takes) money with a reason that is shown in the log – for example for speaking English the whole lesson.

  6. End with travel diaries.

    “📔 Reports” on the teacher page makes a nice travel diary for each group: a map with the route, highlights, hotels, the travel fund and the collection of places. Print it or save it as a PDF – great to show parents!

💡 Money skills: Coaches are cheap, but slow. London is expensive, but the pay is higher there. Let the students find out by themselves – and talk about it afterwards!

Questions and answers

What about privacy?
The students log in with a group code and a PIN – no names, email addresses or accounts. You decide the group names. The data is stored in Firebase in the EU (Belgium). The site uses no tracking and no ads. Do not use the students' real names as group names.
What equipment do I need?
One tablet or computer per group, a big screen for the board, and a printer for the travel log and the login cards. The site works in normal browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari).

For the duels it is smart to pack a “duel box” first: stopwatch, ruler (30 cm), measuring tape and tape, two dice, paper clips, a couple of cups, erasers, rubber bands, a water bottle and a kitchen scale – plus paper and pencils. You get a printable equipment list when you make a new game, and you also find it under the tab “Duels” on the teacher page.
Must the teacher page or the board be open while the students play?
No. The “bank” runs in the cloud. The students only send requests (“we want to buy this ticket”), and the bank calculates the price again and checks the balance. That way nobody can cheat by changing prices or money. You only need the teacher page when you approve travel logs or run duels.
Are the prices real?
The prices are calculated – not live – but they are realistic: the price of a flight follows the distance and the big airports, trains and buses follow the length of the road, and hotels and pay follow the price level of the place. The airlines, train, coach and ferry companies are made up. Prices change a little from day to day, but they are the same for all groups on the same day.
How long does the game last?
You decide with the start and end dates. It works well as an intensive period of 2–4 weeks with several lessons a week. Places are locked automatically 5 days after they are conquered (you can change that), so the game does not become endless ping-pong.
Is the teacher page also in English?
Yes – everything in Across the UK is in English, also the teacher page and the board. The same game exists in Norwegian as Jorda rundt, Europa rundt and Norge rundt.
Does it cost anything?
No. The game is free to use.
